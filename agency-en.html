        Assistant — Ask nowmad
        
        
        
          
        
            
        (06) FAQ
        
            
        What people usually ask us.
        
          
        
          
        
            
        Hi! I'm the nowmad assistant. Tap a question or type your own.
        
          
        
          
        
          
        
            
            
            
          
        
          
        
            
        How much does it cost?
        It depends on what you need and your budget. First we look at your case, then we send you a written proposal with what we'll do, by when and how much it costs. If the budget is tight, we plan the project in stages and start with what has the most impact.
        
            
        Can I hire just the website or just the ads?
        Yes. You can order a single service or combine them: it often pays to do the website and campaigns together so everything is measured end to end.
        
            
        Do I need AI to automate?
        No. Most automations don't use AI: we connect your website, payments, inventory, bookings, invoicing and WhatsApp so that when something happens, the rest takes care of itself. We only add AI when it helps, for example to answer questions.
        
            
        Can I run everything from my phone?
        Yes. We set it up so you can see sales, orders, bookings and alerts from your phone, and get notified when something important happens. All shaped to how your business works.
        
            
        What can I automate with AI?
        Tasks like answering questions, booking appointments, organizing data or generating reports. We'll tell you what makes sense to automate with AI in your case and what's better solved without it.
        
            
        How do I see how the project is going?
        While we build, we show you progress you can see and try, not just at the end.
