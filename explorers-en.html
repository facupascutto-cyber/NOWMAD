FAQ

Good questions.

Is it safe to travel in Argentina?

Yes. Argentina is one of the most visited countries in South America and the places on our routes are well used to international travelers. Use the same common sense you would in any big city, especially in Buenos Aires, and we'll share practical tips for each stop before you go.

Do US citizens need a visa?

No. US citizens can visit Argentina as tourists for up to 90 days with a valid passport. Rules can change, so always check the U.S. State Department's travel page for Argentina before you book your flights.

How do I pay, and in what currency?

All our quotes are in US dollars, per person, with everything included spelled out. To confirm the trip we ask for a deposit of 30% and the balance 45 days before arrival. We'll tell you the available payment methods (card or bank transfer) when we send your quote.

What's included in the price?

Hotels, domestic flights within Argentina, private airport transfers, the tours and English-speaking guides listed in your itinerary, and a local contact on WhatsApp during the whole trip. International flights, travel insurance, most meals and tips are not included unless your itinerary says so.

Can you change a route?

Always. The three routes are starting points: we can add or remove days, switch hotels, combine destinations or slow down the pace. Tell us what you want and we'll send a new version.

Do I need travel insurance?

We strongly recommend it, with medical coverage and trip cancellation. Some cancellation costs can only be recovered through insurance.

When is the best time to go?

It depends on the route. Patagonia is best from November to March, Iguazú is good all year, and Mendoza shines in autumn (March to May) during the harvest. See the season table above, or ask us for your dates.

Cancellation policy

If you need to cancel, let us know in writing (email or WhatsApp). Charges depend on how far in advance you cancel, counted from your arrival date:

  • More than 60 days before: full refund of the deposit, minus non-refundable third-party costs (such as domestic flights already issued).
  • 60 to 31 days before: the 30% deposit is kept.
  • 30 to 15 days before: 50% of the total trip price.
  • Less than 15 days before, or no-show: 100% of the total trip price.

Changing dates instead of cancelling? We'll always try to move your trip at no extra charge, subject to availability. If we ever have to cancel for reasons on our side, you get a full refund. Full details in our terms & conditions.
